THE GLOBAL WATER CRISIS

Water is essential for life, but millions of people face challenges in accessing safe and clean water.

Water Scarcity

Many communities struggle to get enough water for their daily needs.

Water Pollution

Industrial waste, plastic and sewage contaminate rivers and lakes.

Groundwater Depletion

Excessive groundwater extraction reduces underground water resources.

HOW CAN WE CONSERVE WATER?

Small actions can make a big difference. Let's protect our water resources!

Save Every Drop

Turn off taps when not in use.

Rainwater Harvesting

Collect rainwater for future use.

Reuse Water

Reuse suitable household water for plants.
